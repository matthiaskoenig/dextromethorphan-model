This repository provides the dextromethorphan physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PBPK) model: the oral and intravenous administration of dextromethorphan, its O-demethylation to dextrorphan by CYP2D6 (with the activity score of the CYP2D6 diplotype) and by the CYP2D6-independent pathway, the N-demethylation of dextromethorphan and dextrorphan, the glucuronidation of dextrorphan, the distribution in the tissues and the renal excretion of dextromethorphan and its metabolites.
